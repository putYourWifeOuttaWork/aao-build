The ontology, which is the sprint's finding
Altify decomposed its relationship map dimensions and its insight card types into authored question sets years ago and ships them as protected custom labels. Enumerated by exact label from ExternalString, overlay queried and empty in this org, spelling and punctuation untouched. Support is nine propositions, Political Status seven, Coverage three, Buyer Role none at all. Each insight type carries an admission test, three questions for Goal Pressure Initiative Obstacle and four for Solution.
This falsifies the glossary's claim that schema-sourced propositions have no sentence to find. They have eleven. It also means proposition text is stored byte-exact, because organisation on the opportunity map against organization on the account map, jeopardise, a stray hyphen and curly apostrophes are all real and a normalised quote is a paraphrase.
Two structural things a UI walk cannot see. Support is symmetric rather than a tree: a neutral gate, then matched pairs pointing opposite ways, prefer your solution against prefer an alternate, mentoring you against mentoring your competition, plus three more extending the negative side alone. So Enemy is a positively established state with its own evidence questions rather than the far end of a scale. And Political's seven include two that are not about authority at all, track record and information brokering, so reading it as three collapsed political standing into hierarchy.

THE ONE THING BLOCKING BUILD, and it is Matthew's
The labels are unreachable from Apex. ExternalString is a Tooling API object and Apex SOQL cannot see it, and System.Label is shut too because 2,576 of the 2,930 ALTF labels are protected, including every guided question. Only 354 are public. So discovery cannot read the ontology at runtime from inside the org.
The question text is a property of the package version rather than of the org, so every org on a version has identical text and only the localization overlay is org-specific. Recommended and not yet ruled: ship the packaged text keyed by package version as the default, and make the overlay read an optional upgrade for a customer who has customised, which is per-customer configuration rather than an architectural fork, the same shape as polling versus Change Data Capture. The alternative is a Tooling callout through a Named Credential to the org itself, which buys full fidelity and costs one setup action plus an InfoSec conversation about Tooling scope. AAO_PeopleOntology is built with the seam in the right place, so this is a sourcing decision and not an architectural one. Nothing else waits on it.

People is closed except the persona emission, which is owed. Problems is closed. Politics is next.

Politics, and its shape is already known
There is no authored ontology for influence or conflict. Confirmed by read rather than by memory: sixteen labels across both families and every one is a UI string, plus two mode help texts that are interaction instructions rather than propositions. So Politics is authored by us from zero, landing Inferred_Pending until ratified, and with nothing to discover it is simpler than either charter before it, no vendor ontology, no account-versus-opportunity split, no sparse numbering, no overlay.
Opening position, worked out but not ruled. Influence has three citable shapes and the strongest is the one nobody reaches for. A third party reports it, which is the common case and inherits doubt about whether they would know. The influencer reports it, which is self-serving and weak alone. And the influenced person reports it, I need to check with Tom, let me run it past Sarah, which is first person and unambiguous and said constantly on calls without anyone thinking of it as political disclosure. Excluded on purpose: observed deference, because that is reading the room and it is the behavioural-pattern inference already forbidden for Support. Conflict is weaker, and the line would be reported establishes and observed never, since two people disagreeing once is a disagreement rather than a conflict. Whether reported conflict alone is worth building, or whether conflict joins Adaptability and Decision Orientation as human-only, is open. The subject is a pair and the speaker is usually neither half, which is a speaker-requirement shape we do not have, and it should need presence rather than rank because a junior person often knows the politics better. Confirmation by concordance, one report standing weak and two from different people confirming, which reuses the Problems rule.

Ruled across the sprint, all in corrections v2.9
Ontology owns every rung, so Support is what Altify's tree computes from its propositions, and the one-per-source law and the clamp are withdrawn for ontology-backed dimensions, surviving as the inference fallback where no ontology exists. Coverage is a count of our own Sources and never reaches a model. Insight admission tests are guidance rather than gates and their text stays out of the reader's input. Guidance goes only where we hold evidence. Dedup is type-blind. Machine confirmation is an autonomy level. Solutions from line items are state and their edges are evidence. The missing-relation flag is keyed on deal plus relation kind. Cold seeding is per dimension: Support resets to Neutral because its questions are about this deal's solution, Political carries under a one-year bound, Buyer Role never seeds, Coverage needs no seed. Contention is unbounded but aggregated. Flags get no charter because nothing may gate a flag being raised. Contention splits three ways and the configurable part is authored at setup and frozen. Persona is the People charter's fourth emission, additive only, never removed. Opportunity and Account are never written, absolute; Contact stays toggleable. A flag ages from when its question became askable. Anything hung off an after-insert can turn our defect into lost evidence.
