What happened, 30–31 July, ruled by Matthew in the sandbox-build sessions

- **The naming correction, the big one.** The current-state row is the **Answer** (upserted, uniquely keyed, human precedence enforced here). A **Claim** is one establishment from one piece of evidence, immutable, carrying verdict-before and verdict-after, four keys, two clocks. **Journal Event is retired as an entity** — its keys, clocks and subject identity moved onto Claim; replaying claims rebuilds the answers, and that replay is the exit test. **Claim Basis** added: a junction recording what a claim rests on beyond its own quotes, cited values frozen at claim time, master-detail to Claim. Entity count is fifteen. The full argument and each document's owed changes are in the corrections doc.
- **Field-level design closed** for Source, Evidence Contract, Candidate, Answer, Claim, Claim Basis and Flag, every field naming its writer and reader. Guidance's topology is ruled (own object, offer not demand, computed on change and held stable, suppression changes no state); its field table is Wave 2. The seller Surface is deliberately after that.
- **The build is real and deployed** to sandbox `altify--aossb2` (org `00DWD00000DV7iT2AT`). 83 AAO tests green. Proven in the org: accumulation (transcript one lands UNVERIFIED with receipts, transcript two flips the answer TRUE, the first claim never edited), replay exact on every deal, the speaker-rank negative case (same words from the seller land UNVERIFIED, outcome Downgraded), and an unsegmented Notion-style artifact.
- **The live path exists:** an after-insert trigger on our Source object enqueues a Queueable that adjudicates. Arrival and adjudication are deliberately separate transactions, so nothing can roll back the arrival of evidence.
- **Three synthetic demo deals** (Tungsten Rehearsal TRUE, seller-said-it UNVERIFIED, AAO Demo - Live). `AAO_Synthetic__c` is ratified on every object: written only by seeders, required by purge, and excluded by every aggregating, streaming or projecting reader — the third universal filter after publication state and current-deal exclusion.
- **Learned from the org, permanent:** `caseSensitive` requires `unique`, so five hash fields lost the flag and the frozen composers enforce lowercase hex instead; `commit` is an Apex reserved word; a parameter named `json` silently shadows the JSON class. Five collisions in total as of 1 Aug 2026 — see the field tables for the list, which separates the ones the compiler refuses from the two it accepts silently.
- **Also ratified:** the internal-person rule on Claim (the internal participant who spoke in the cited spans, else one present on the call, never the deal owner); `Opportunity` as a sixth subject type (discriminator value only, no new lookup); contention flags freeze their historical basis in fields on Flag, not via Claim Basis, because an aggregate over memory has no core row for a typed lookup to hold.
- **Known missing, deliberately:** discovery (the six demo propositions were seeded directly into our contract object; Altify's own rubric tables are confirmed present and empty in the sandbox, which is the customer condition, and reading from them is next); model calls (charters stay behind Gate 1's bar until round two clears — nothing a model wrote exists anywhere); projection into ALTF objects; flag raising; guidance fields; Surfacing.
