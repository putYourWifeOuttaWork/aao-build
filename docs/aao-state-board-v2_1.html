The road from here

Everything that has to exist before this is a product, in build order, in plain words. Where we are: Act I, step 5.

ACT I · Finish the spine — one deal, evidence to flags, everything real

1Objects + lawsThe eight core objects with their triggers: things can be written, never faked, always replayed.
2Model in the loopA real model proposes, real checks judge it, and we measured it against known truth.
3DiscoveryThe system reads the customer's own question tables and builds its contracts from them. Works in an org it has never seen.
4Flags that raiseLive: reds stand from deal creation, TRUE clears them on screen, seller-said-it holds its reds at full coverage. Aging/escalation still owed.
5The other chartersPeople, problems, politics: reading who was on the call, what hurts, who influences whom — writing maps, not just answers.
6Wave 2 objectsNotes, links, shadow people, personas' history, non-establishment rules: the seven remaining entities.
7Ratification + setup passesThe human yes/no surface, and the once-per-rubric passes that propose speaker rules, routes, and elements for approval.
8Real transcriptsTungsten-length calls, the small-talk boundary, cardinality guards under load. Where the charters earn their grades.

ACT II · Make it real — many deals, history, and the write-back

9The memory plane (your buckets)Claims stream to Data 360 and live forever under keys: two-year lookups, cold starts, deal history — one query, no re-reading.
10Contention vs historyThe person who was an enemy three years ago just became your decision maker. The system says so, with receipts.
11Real ingestionThe Data 360 door wired: connectors, the Ingestion API, then email. Notes last, designed with Toby.
12ProjectionVerified answers written back into Altify's own panels: watermarked, toggleable, humans always win.
13Surface + guidanceOne current-state record per deal that briefs, agents, and dashboards all read. Guidance held stable between changes.

ACT III · Make it a product — other people's hands

14Autonomy levels liveThe three-level human-approval dial, yellow flags, decline-with-consequences.
15Internal pilotAltify's own live deals, a few sellers, projection on. Where efficacy gets measured for real.
16Package + security posture2GP, the ALTF namespace endgame, the InfoSec story formalized. Decisions with Toby.
17Pilot customers → DreamforceOne or two orgs that are not ours. September.
