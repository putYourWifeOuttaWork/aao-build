## Before the room · two decisions and one setup

**1 · Activation scope, yours.** Either activate `AAO_Demo_Run` as the Opportunity record page (every
Opportunity gets the paste panel), or leave it unassigned and open it deliberately in App Builder
preview. **Deliberate-open is the conservative default** and is what the stamp recommends for
tomorrow. Activation is one click in App Builder and reversible.

**2 · Which deal.** The harness deal is warm and already carries a board, so a run there shows
accumulation against standing evidence. A **fresh throwaway** shows a clean board filling from
nothing, which reads better cold. **Never Wells Fargo** — the instrument gets no rehearsal, ever.

**3 · Rehearse with different bytes than you demo with.** The scope-key guard refuses identical text
against the same deal, correctly, and it will refuse in the room if the demo re-pastes the rehearsal
transcript. Two distinct transcripts, or a fresh deal for the live run.

## The paste, and what it needs

Three fields. **The roster is required and that is by law, not by omission:** the side split comes
from attendee metadata and the transcript body may never supply it (88th stamp). A connector fills
this from the meeting platform; a paste surface has to ask.

```
Who was on the call?   dana, Dana Whitfield, dana.whitfield@opentext.com
                       marcus, Marcus Reyes, marcus.reyes@northbank.example

Paste                  dana<TAB>Where are you feeling the pain today?
                       marcus<TAB>We lose about six hours a week chasing prior approvals...
```

Speaker key, then a TAB, then the words. **The email decides internal versus external**, so the
seller's line needs a seller domain or the map will carry them as a buyer.

## What they will see, in order, and roughly when

Eight stages, each appearing **only once the run has journalled it** — nothing on that surface is
estimated, and a stage that has not run reads WAITING. Measured on the two real runs: **call 0 ~5s,
each read ~7s, model leg ~5s, verify ~7s, join ~1s, projection ~1.5s, cards ~4–8s. Total ~38s** on
a short paste; the poll refreshes every three seconds.

Then the ledger: words located, people identified, upheld by the blind verifier, **refused with
reasons**, claims, answers, cards.

**The refusals are the part worth narrating.** A surface that only shows what stood tells you what
the machine believes; the refusal count is where trust is earned.

## Three things that will happen, so none of them is a surprise

**1 · A restatement WILL duplicate a card.** If the script has somebody say the same thing twice in
different words, the board gets two cards. That is the hundred-eighth stamp's open decision, not a
bug to hide: **narrate it as the next build** or keep restatement out of the script. Live specimen
already on the harness board — `6 hrs/week chasing prior approvals` beside `Sign-off chase: ~1
day/week, no change`.

**2 · A mention of somebody the org has no Contact for raises a flag rather than guessing.** That is
the product working, and it clears by doing the work — never by dismissing.

**3 · If a stage fails, the surface says so with the reason** and stops. That was built deliberately:
a demo that cannot show a failure lies by omission.

## What is still unproven, stated plainly

**`startRun`, the three-second poll, the stage list filling live, and finish-and-stop under a real
render cycle.** The App Builder canvas executes no Apex, so all four are proved only from the
runtime. **The rehearsal is what proves them** — do one before the room, on the harness deal or a
throwaway, and if anything is wrong it surfaces there rather than in front of an audience.
