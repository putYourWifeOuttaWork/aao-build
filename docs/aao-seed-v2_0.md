Known hazards for the charter work

Blind means blind to the first verdict, never to the question: the reader receives the proposition, elements, interpretation, and spans, nothing else. Where a model interprets a question beyond its text, the interpretation is its own field, travels to the blind reader, lands on the claim. Seller speech is admissible in a citation set; the assertion-carrying span obeys the speaker rule; one assent cannot carry a compound antecedent. Unsegmented sources cannot satisfy speaker-gated propositions. A charter never sees the mirror's labels, only structural slots. Cardinality bounds on every creation path.
