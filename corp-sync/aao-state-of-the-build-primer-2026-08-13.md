## What AAO is, in two sentences

Altify Always On is a Salesforce-native layer that reads a deal's real conversations and verifies, per opportunity, which necessary conditions are actually true, binding every establishment to the verbatim words that produced it. It verifies, it never predicts: a value lands only with its quote, its byte offset, and an unbroken evidence trail behind it, and a human edit beats the machine forever.

## The laws that do not move

Production is read-only, unconditionally. No dependency on any Altify package version; the system runs with Altify absent. Nothing of ours lands on objects we do not own; on vendor objects, nothing that already exists is ever modified or deleted, and our own rows arrive add-only. Every value walks back to verbatim evidence; wrong records retire in place; nothing is deleted; no dismiss button exists anywhere. Claims are insert-only. Call 3 stays blind. Human edits beat the machine forever. Corrections go into documents, never chat.
