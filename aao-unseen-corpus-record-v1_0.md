## 1 · The sweep and its verdict

The v2.2 ruling asked for an enterprise-grade 30-to-45-minute B2B sales-call transcript, preferring found real speech from the web. The sweep ran about twenty-eight probes: search across the sales-training world (Gong, 30MPC, trainer channels, self-published calls), public-record verbatim transcripts (Nevada PEBP, Pittsburgh CMPTF, CalPERS, NYC Council, municipal platforms), caption-based council vendor demos (Flock, Axon, ERP finalist genres), podcast archives, and public datasets (HuggingFace, Kaggle, academic corpora).

**Verdict: full, real, named-speaker, deal-stage B2B sales conversations effectively do not exist on the open web.** Four load-bearing findings:

1. **Private sales calls are published only as snippets or inside gated libraries.** No full call with consent was found.
2. **Court-reported public-body transcripts have named speakers and real vendor segments, but every pre-award sales presentation found retreated into closed session or an untranscribed subcommittee.** Verified by inventorying four transcripts: PEBP 7 Dec 2023 (Carrum Health segment moved to closed session), PEBP 23 May 2024 (UMR post-award remediation; consultant-led PBM market check), CMPTF 2 May 2024 (incumbent consultant only), CMPTF 5 Sep 2024 (private-credit selection reported post-hoc; the finalist presentations happened at an untranscribed 12 Aug 2024 subcommittee).
3. **Council vendor demos are real and deal-shaped but exist as unlabeled closed captions.** Assigning speakers ourselves is inferred attribution; the normalization research (v0.1, section 1.4) says identity-tier inference is unpassed, and the charter runs unanchored attribution at Any_Participant power. Not fixture-grade.
4. **One verified full, uncut, speaker-labeled real conversation:** The Pitch, S10 E12, "Handle: The Uncut Pitch." Real founder, real investors, real money, published deliberately with a full labeled transcript. Investor-pitch genre, not a solution sale.

This scarcity is itself evidence for the training-set hazard: unseen customer speech at fixture grade is not obtainable from the web. ~~The production pull remains the only path to positive foreign measurement.~~ **[v1.1: and it happened. Section 4b.]**
