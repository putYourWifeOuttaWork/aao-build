Altify Always On (AAOS) · what each thing on the screen is

Six objects. The names below are exactly what the related lists and the pipeline tab call them.
Sourcewords arrive, frozen → Candidateproposals checked → Claimreceipt written → Answertruth updated → Flaggaps escalate → Projectionhanded back to Altify (off)

Evidence Contract

AAO_Evidence_Contract__c
The question, as data

One record per question, per version of its wording: the sentence, what would establish it in the methodology author's own words, its parts (funds approved · allocated to this initiative · this fiscal year), and who must be the speaker.

The machine never invents questions. It answers these. In a customer org these are assembled by reading their own rubric.

In the demoAAO_T1, Budget Confirmed, three parts, speaker must be the decision maker.

Source

AAO_Source__c
The words, frozen

A transcript, email or note, normalised once and never edited. Every quote anywhere in the system is byte-checked against this exact text, so the bytes are locked by trigger.

Carries who was on the call, when the evidence occurred (not when we read it), and how it was diarized.

In the demoTwo calls, eleven days apart on the evidence clock, processed minutes apart.

Candidate

AAO_Candidate__c
The proposal, and its fate

One row per question considered per pass, including the questions the call said nothing about — those rows say so. Carries what was proposed, every check it passed, and why it died if it died.

This is why "did it even look" is answerable, and why the abstention rate is measurable. Internal only: sellers never see candidates.

In the demoSix considered per pass; five say nobody-said, one carries the budget claim.

Claim

AAO_Claim__c
The receipt · never edited

One establishment from one piece of evidence: what the answer was before, what it became, the verbatim quotes, the speaker, and both clocks.

Claim one says UNVERIFIED forever, even after the answer flips TRUE. That is progress staying visible instead of being overwritten. Replaying the claims rebuilds every answer exactly.

In the demonull → UNVERIFIED, then UNVERIFIED → TRUE, and the first claim untouched.

Answer

AAO_Answer__c
What is true right now

One row per question. TRUE, FALSE or UNVERIFIED, nothing else. Carries the accumulated quotes and the coverage: which parts are established, which part is still missing.

A human edit here wins forever. The machine is never allowed to overwrite a person; the strongest thing it can do is disagree in writing, with a flag.

In the demoBudget Confirmed goes UNVERIFIED after call one, TRUE after call two. On the seller-said-it deal: all parts covered and still UNVERIFIED, because the seller said them.

Flag

AAO_Flag__c
What is still unmet

Type is set at birth and decides how it clears: methodological clears by evidence only, never by dismissal; contention clears by written acknowledgement that transfers the risk; ratification clears by approve or decline.

Empty today because raising is not built yet. When it lands, every gating question stands red from the day the deal opens, and evidence is the only thing that lowers the count.

In the demoZero, honestly: the next deterministic piece, not a gap in the idea.
1. Every establishment carries the verbatim words that produced it. 2. Humans always win; the machine may only disagree in writing. 3. Replaying the receipts rebuilds the truth exactly.
Today the meaning is staged and every piece of machinery around it is the shipping path: verification, commit, accumulation, replay. Model charters land behind Gate 1. Projection into Altify's own panels is greyed on the pipeline until it ships: dynamic, watermarked, toggleable.
