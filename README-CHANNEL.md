**CORP sessions (any Claude session in the corporate org)** read everything here and VERIFY: check exports against READMEs, arithmetic against the counting laws, receipts against ledger bytes. They write ONLY under `corp-notes/` and `review/verify-corp-*/`. They never write the ledger, the law docs, the org, or CODE's run folders. A corp finding reaches the ledger through design, like any report.

The ledger's newest stamp is at the top of `CODE-INBOX.md`. Read it before acting. If your task is not authorized by bytes you can quote from it, write your question into `corp-notes/` and stop.
