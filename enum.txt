## INSIGHT DEFINITIONS  (10)

GOAL_DEF_AM
    An Account Goal is an end result which an Executive needs to achieve, ideally with a measurable outcome and a specified time frame.

GOAL_DEF_OM
    A Goal is an end result which the Approver and Decision Maker need to achieve specific to the initiative, ideally with a measurable outcome and a specified time frame.

INITIATIVE_DEF_AM
    An Account Initiative is a project created to address the Pressure(s) and achieve the Goal(s).

INITIATIVE_DEF_OM
    An Initiative is a project created to address the Pressure(s) and achieve the Goal(s) specific to this opportunity.

OBSTACLE_DEF_AM
    An Account Obstacle is an internal operational problem, typically with either organization, process, culture, skills, or technology. Something that is broken, or doesnt exist, and needs to be enabled during the Initiative.

OBSTACLE_DEF_OM
    An Obstacle is an internal operational problem, typically with either organization, process, culture, skills, or technology. Something that is broken, or doesnt exist, and needs to be enabled during the Initiative.

PRESSURE_DEF_AM
    An Account Pressure is an internal and/or external business issue that significantly impacts achieving the Goal(s), and serves as a driver for Initiative(s).

PRESSURE_DEF_OM
    A Pressure is an internal and/or external business issue that significantly impacts achieving the Goal(s), and serves as the driver for the Initiative(s) specific to this opportunity.

SOLUTION_DEF_AM
    A set of capabilities, products, or services that help remove the Obstacles - enabling the success of the Initiative, relieving the Pressures, and contributing to achieving the Goals.

SOLUTION_DEF_OM
    A set of capabilities, products, or services that help remove the Obstacles - enabling the success of the Initiative, relieving the Pressures, and contributing to achieving the Goals.
