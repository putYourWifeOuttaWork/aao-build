## 1 · ABSENT · six rows, one missing proposition

**The Political Structure family, declared, in full:**

- `AAO_POL_PS1` — *Does this person execute projects after they are approved?*
- `AAO_POL_PS2` — *Is this person called on to make it happen?*
- `AAO_POL_PS3` — *Do others seek this person out for advice and direction?*
- `AAO_POL_PS4` — *Does this person have a track record of success in implementing projects that deliver value?*

All six rows ran under **PS2**, and PS2's seven words are the whole of the failure. Read the four
together and the gap is structural rather than lexical: **every one of the four asks about
standing — authority, being relied upon, being sought out, having a record. Not one asks whether
the person is operating inside the structure.**

| row | span | the note's key | why the set cannot express it |
|---|---|---|---|
| poq1 | @100 | forwarding the MSA to legal | doing the work is not being called on to do it; PS1 wants an *approved project*, and an MSA in redline is not one |
| poq2 | @370 | asks others to expedite, expects an update | PS2 runs the other way: it asks who is called on, and Jefferson is the one calling |
| poq3 | @9047 | describes the process without the action being assigned | the refusal reason **is** the missing proposition: describing the ladder is the fact, and nothing asks for it |
| poq6 | @10375 | volunteers to raise something with Neeraja | self-initiated, so nobody called on him |
| poq11 | @12434 | explicitly disclaims being the approver | the disclaimer's own affirmative content is a structure fact, and no question receives it |
| poq12 | @11484 | *"I can only ask the question"*, relaying to others | same shape |

**Why this is ABSENT and not PS2 being too narrow.** Widening PS2 to admit these six would make
it mean "participates in the process," which swallows PS1 and PS3 whole and leaves the family
with four questions and one meaning. The authority test PS2 currently performs is a real
distinction worth keeping: **being the decider and knowing the ladder are different facts about a
person, and a seller acts on them differently.** The repair is a fifth proposition, not a wider
second one.

**poq3 is the specimen I would put in front of Matthew**, ahead of poq11. Its refusal reason is
*"describes a generic process without assigning the action to this person"* — which is a precise,
correct description of content the contract set has no question for. The verifier is not failing;
it is reporting an absence in its own instructions.

**A consequence worth stating before anyone writes the new proposition:** it must not be written
so that describing *any* process establishes structure membership, or the negative control will
establish political structure on an investor pitch. The narrowing that keeps it honest is
probably the person's own involvement in the ladder they describe, which poq3 has and a bystander
would not.

## 2 · PRESENT AND TOO NARROW · three rows, two contracts, two different narrowings

**poq7, poq8 — `AAO_POL_PS1`, *"Does this person execute projects after they are approved?"***

The narrowing is the temporal clause **"after they are approved."** Ryan saying he gets to start
this one and is looking forward to getting into the sandbox and building it out is execution he
will perform on a project not yet approved. The contract is also written in the habitual present,
which reads as a disposition, while the words are a specific forward commitment.

**Exposure if reworded:** PS1 carries standing assertions from the training set. Loosening the
temporal clause changes what PS1 means on every one of them. This is the class the twenty-third
stamp warns about and it should be re-graded, not just re-run.

**buq6 — `AAO_BR_EVAL`, *"Did this person weigh what is being offered - comparing options,
pressing on capability against a need, or probing price, terms or fit?"***

The narrowing is the **enumeration**. Jefferson correcting the legal entity name for document
consistency is scrutiny of the agreement's terms, and the list offers "price, terms or fit" but
the verifier read "terms" as commercial terms rather than contract accuracy.

**This is §8 defect 2 returning and the tuned-behaviour law's first confirmed instance**, recorded
repaired at v0.6 and verified only on the training set. Worth naming plainly: **the previous
repair was not wrong, it was unverified**, and the same repair applied again with the same
evidence base would earn the same status.

## 3 · PRESENT AND CORRECTLY APPLIED · nine rows, and no contract change is the finding

**Five sentiment rows — `AAO_PS_1`, *"Do this person's own words voice how they stand toward us
and our solution?"*** (seq1, seq2, seq3, seq4, seq6)

The declared text says *toward us and our solution* in its own words. Every one of the five notes
refuses on exactly that clause. **There is no divergence between the contract and its
application, so there is nothing here CODE can repair without changing what MENTOR means.**
Per the twenty-third stamp, this is Matthew's ruling and nothing goes near it. Recorded as
standing assertions that currently fail, visibly.

**poq4, poq5 — `AAO_POL_OPS1`, *"Does this person collect and provide information to people of
power and influence?"***

Present tense, and it reads habitual. One instance of wanting a message socialised is not a
practice. **Design already called the verifier defensible here and I agree**; I would not widen
OPS1, because a contract that fires on a single instance of passing information along will fire
on most sentences in most calls.

**One fact the grouping did not carry, and it belongs to the Board's open item rather than to
this report: poq5 and poq6 are the same span, @10375, the same person.** poq5 refused under
OPS1 and poq6 refused under PS2, and both were graded TRUE under labels the ontology treats as
mutually exclusive. seq4 is on that span too. Whatever is decided about OPS1, that span is one
utterance answering three questions and it is the cleanest available specimen for the
row-dependency finding.

**buq9 — `AAO_BR_USER`, *"Does this person use, or will they use, the products or services being
bought?"***

Ryan saying Jefferson goes to the next adventure and he starts this one implies he will be the
user. **buq8, on the same contract, was upheld and graded TRUE** on words that name the use
outright. The contract fires when the words carry the fact and declines when it must be inferred,
which is the line the byte-exact discipline draws everywhere else. **Widening it licenses
inference, and inference is the one thing the verification layer exists to refuse.**

**deq3 — `AAO_DC_N`, *"Did this person, in their own words, state a condition, capability or
outcome and tie it to evaluating, choosing or proceeding with a solution?"***

Legal's general preference plus a pending confirmation genuinely does not tie a condition to
proceeding. **On its own text the refusal is right.** The defect on this row is not the contract:
it is that **deq4 upheld FORMAL on the identical bytes**, verifying a property of a criterion
whose existence the set refused. That is the row-dependency blind spot and it is a proposal, not
a wording change.

## 4 · The caveat, carried as design instructed rather than left implicit

**An unknown share of 0-of-18 is STAGE, not generalisation, and this run cannot separate them.**

a23 is a late-stage contracting call: routing paperwork, chasing legal, naming ladders,
disclaiming authority, handing over. The classification above is stable against that — an absent
proposition is absent whatever the register — but **the two TOO-NARROW findings are exactly the
kind that a different stage could reverse.** PS1's temporal clause looks narrow on a call where
nothing is approved yet; on a post-signature call it may be precisely right. BR_EVAL's
enumeration looks narrow on a call about document accuracy; on a discovery call it may never
bind.

**So the three-row TOO-NARROW set is the part of this report I would not build from yet, and the
six-row ABSENT set is the part I would.** Additive costs nothing if the stage caveat turns out to
matter; a wording change on PS1 or BR_EVAL costs a re-grade of every standing assertion on those
contracts, and would have to be paid twice if the mixed-stage transcripts then say something
different.

**This moves Matthew's production transcripts from load-bearing to blocking for two specific
rows**, and for nothing else in this report.
