## What this does not license

- No precision or recall number may be derived from this table.
- No contract wording may be tuned toward the human column. That would be tuning to a target the
  machine was never shown, which is the training-set problem with extra steps.
- The three role disagreements are **not** three misses. On the evidence available to it, the
  machine's reading is defensible on every one.

## The measurement that would be a grade, named so nobody mistakes this for it

A real comparison needs the human to build a map **from the same transcript, without prior
knowledge, blind to the machine's output.** That instrument does not exist and is not cheap. Until
it does, this satellite is the honest substitute and is labelled as one.
