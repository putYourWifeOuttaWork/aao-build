## The rulings this draft is built from (forty-second stamp, Matthew's, 11 August)

Admission carries no materiality judgment: a card is admitted because a person genuinely said the thing. Type matching is the judgment that matters. Dedup-aware writing is the standing discipline. The card face is one sentence or less; citations and notes ride the detail fields. Goal-blocking is plain text. Politics slims to people-to-insights; influence and conflict are deferred.

## The Problems contracts · four types, one family

Family: `Problems`. Codes `AAO_PB_GOAL`, `AAO_PB_PRESS`, `AAO_PB_INIT`, `AAO_PB_OBST`. All four ride the same two comprehensive reads as questions, verify in a family-homogeneous batch, and land at the join per the thirty-first stamp's one-pass law. Contracts declare their family via `AAO_Family__c` per the forty-third stamp's routing build, so no Apex edit is needed to mint them.

**GOAL** — Did this person, in their own words, state an outcome they or their organization want to achieve? A goal is a desired future state, wanted, not yet true. Company goals and personal goals both admit; the card records whose goal it is in the text. The vendor's decision-maker-personal filter is dropped by ruling. Guidance for the verifier: wanting is the test; a description of the current state is not a goal; a vendor capability the speaker likes is not their goal.

**PRESSURE** — Did this person, in their own words, describe a force acting on them or their organization that demands response? The vendor's categories (Financial, Operational, Customer, Partner, Technology, Supplier, Market, Competitive) are guidance for recognition, never a gate. Funding or sponsorship consequences are not required, by ruling. Guidance: a pressure pushes; if the words describe something they merely want, it is a Goal; if the words describe something in their way, it is an Obstacle. Where a pressure blocks a stated goal, the card text names which goal, in plain words.

**INITIATIVE** — Did this person, in their own words, describe an effort, project, or program underway or decided, intended to change something? Funded-and-prioritized is not required, by ruling; the card records funding or sponsorship words when they were actually said. Guidance: an initiative is action taken or committed; intent without commitment is a Goal.

**OBSTACLE** — Did this person, in their own words, describe a task, situation, or process that stands in the way of something they are trying to do? The vendor's substantial-value test is dropped by ruling. Where the obstacle blocks a stated goal or more than one, the card text names them, in plain words. Guidance: an obstacle blocks; a complaint about the world with nothing blocked is sentiment, not an obstacle.

**Type matching is the verified judgment.** The verifier judges: do these words, from this speaker, genuinely establish this type? Same blindness, same byte-located quotes, same refusal discipline as People. Cross-type confusion (a Goal upheld as a Pressure) is the calibration target, the analog of People's role contracts.

## The card, as written

One sentence or less on the face, in the speaker's own terms, understandable at a glance. Citations in the detail fields, the ruled plain style: date plus speaker, "on a demo call June eighth," no URLs, no record ids. The full receipt lives on the claim, as everywhere. Confirmation counting as already ruled: twice in one call confirms; once lands unconfirmed; a later call's re-mention notes the date and confirms; a different person reinforcing appends attribution.

## Dedup-aware writing, the discipline, mechanized

Before any card writes, the writer reads the deal's existing cards and compares meaning within type: same type, same meaning is REINFORCEMENT (parent card kept, count prepended, reiteration appended, a Claim with outcome Reinforced behind it); same type, different meaning is a new card; a card whose meaning CONTRADICTS an existing card is written and FLAGGED beside the pair, never silently landed and never silently suppressed. Nothing overwrites an existing card's text except the reinforcement mechanics; human-authored cards are never touched, only reinforced beside, per the annotation law. Completely different language is a different insight, as ruled.

## The thin Politics slice · people to insights

No influence edges, no conflict edges, deferred whole. What builds: the PERSON-TO-INSIGHT connection, who said it and who reinforced it, which attribution already substantially carries. The landing surface is the vendor's own card-to-contact relation where present (`ALTF__Insight_Card_Contact__c`, subject to the org-ownership rails: read and write data rows, never metadata on their objects), with our answer ledger authoritative behind it as everywhere. Both-endpoints law rides: a person-to-insight link writes only when the person's map row and the card both exist, this run or standing; a link with a missing endpoint is held with its evidence.

## What is deliberately not in this draft

Influence and conflict edges. Account-level insight variants. Solution cards and the solution edge (the recovered ontology's fifth lane, still owed its own pass). Card enrichment beyond the one-sentence face. The Decision Orientation family (demanded three times in grading, still undeclared, recorded not built).
